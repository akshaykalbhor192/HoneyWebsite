## Journey
1. Recognition: sunlight through a forest; this honey comes from somewhere real.
2. Origin: bees on wild blossom; it is the forest, not a farm.
3. Hands: the people who harvest it.
4. The pour (peak): the visitor pours the honey themselves.
5. Substance: raw comb, what "never heated" means.
6. Range: choose your honey by colour, light to dark.
7. Commitment: one action, "Shop the harvest".

## Feeling curve
1. Calm wonder: layered forest light, jar framed in a temple arch drifting forward.
2. Curiosity: a sentence lights up word by word as you read it with the wheel.
3. Respect: lateral pan across harvesters' photos.
4. (authored silence: short dark breath before the peak)
5. Delight, control: the stream of honey follows your scroll, grams climb, jar fills. Largest span.
6. Trust: macro comb opens through a hexagon wipe.
7. Playful appetite: jars light to dark, hover tastes each.
8. Resolve: one golden arch, one button, held still.
